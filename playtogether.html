How to play

Collect every cheese to open the gate, then run through the ring. Fastest time wins.

Keyboard

  • WS go and reverse   AD steer
  • Drag to look around, scroll to zoom

Touch

  • Left thumb: joystick. Push to go, pull down to reverse
  • Right side: drag to look around

Racing

  • Your best run comes back as a ghost to beat
  • Sectors turn green for a personal best, purple for the day's best
